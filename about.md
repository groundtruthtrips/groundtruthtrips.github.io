Ground Truth brings computer science students together with biodiversity and conservation practitioners. Students learn about conservation challenges in the field and explore how computing can contribute to addressing them.

Ground Truth is a study trip offered under the Centre for Computing for Social Good and Philanthropy (CCSGP) Study Trip Programme at the NUS School of Computing. It introduces a new land-based trip format to the programme, with each edition centred on biodiversity and conservation.

This site covers Ground Truth only. The official CCSGP website is at [www.ccsgp.comp.nus.edu.sg](https://www.ccsgp.comp.nus.edu.sg/).

Each edition takes place in collaboration with local partners and combines field activities, learning, cross-cultural exchange, and project work.
